\begin{abstract}
We evaluate N-Queens using PySAT/Glucose3 with pairwise and sequential CNF
encodings, CPLEX MIP, CPLEX CP Optimizer, and Gurobi. The original comparison benchmark has
three trials for each supported configuration at $N=1,2,3,4,8,16$, producing
72 records. PySAT and CP Optimizer completed their records; CPLEX MIP reached
the Community Edition model-size limit at $N=16$. Gurobi was added in a
separate follow-up; OR-Tools remains unavailable.
\end{abstract}
\maketitle

\section{Introduction and problem formulation}
N-Queens asks for one placement of $N$ queens on an $N\times N$ board with no
shared row, column, or diagonal. The SAT variable $x_{r,c}$ means that row $r$
contains a queen in column $c$. Row and column exactly-one constraints and
binary diagonal exclusions define the CNF model.

\section{Methods}
The SAT implementation uses pairwise AMO and Sinz sequential AMO encodings.
The medium matrix additionally evaluates binary and commander AMO encodings
with Glucose3 and CaDiCaL. CPLEX MIP and Gurobi use binary cell variables, exact
row and column constraints, and pairwise diagonal inequalities. CPLEX CP
Optimizer uses one integer column variable per row, an all-different
constraint, and pairwise diagonal inequalities. All returned solutions pass
the independent \texttt{validate_solution} validator. The OR-Tools adapter is
implemented but the package is unavailable. Gurobi was unavailable when the
original comparison artifact was generated and is covered by the follow-up.

\section{Experimental setup}
The comparison schema records instance size, method, solver and version,
encoding, trial, timeout, model-build time, solve time, total wall time, model
size, status, validation, and error. The benchmark used Python 3.11.9 on
Windows 10, a 10-second timeout, and three trials. Total wall time includes the
adapter call; build and solve times are recorded separately. Medians exclude
ERROR and TIMEOUT records. The artifacts are
\texttt{results/raw/benchmark_comparison.json},
\texttt{results/raw/benchmark_comparison.csv}, and
\texttt{results/raw/benchmark_comparison_summary.json}.

\section{Correctness and results}
The latest test run passed 25 tests. The historical SAT benchmark contains 80
records: 60 SAT and 20 UNSAT. The new comparison benchmark contains 72 records:
PySAT has 24 SAT and 12 UNSAT; CPLEX CP Optimizer has 12 SAT and 6 UNSAT; and
CPLEX MIP has 9 SAT, 6 UNSAT, and 3 ERROR. The CPLEX MIP errors at $N=16$
are explicit Community Edition failures: 256 variables and 2512 constraints
exceed the 1000-variable/1000-constraint promotional limits.

The sequential encoding was faster than pairwise at $N=16$ in this run, while
their runtimes were similar at $N=8$. CPLEX CP Optimizer completed $N=16$,
whereas CPLEX MIP did not because of the promotional limit. These are
environment-specific observations, not a universal ranking. The historical
SAT-only results through $N=64$ remain separate from this comparison artifact.

\section{Approved medium encoding matrix}
An additional matrix was executed after adding Binary and Commander AMO
encodings and the CaDiCaL backend. The correctness matrix contains 40 records:
four SAT encodings with each of Glucose3 and CaDiCaL, plus CPLEX MIP and CP
Optimizer, on $N=1,2,3,4$. The performance matrix contains 87 records over
$N=4,8,16$, three repetitions, and a 10-second timeout. CPLEX MIP was measured
only at $N=4,8$; $N=16$ was excluded after the verified Community Edition limit.

The new artifacts are
\texttt{results/raw/benchmark\_medium\_correctness.*} and
\texttt{results/raw/benchmark\_medium\_performance.*}. Every SAT or FEASIBLE
solution passed the independent validator. The normalized statuses preserve
SAT, UNSAT, FEASIBLE, TIMEOUT, ERROR, and LICENSE\_LIMIT, while
\texttt{raw\_status} retains the backend status. Binary and Commander were
evaluated as additional SAT encodings, not as different problem models.

Gurobi was verified separately using the existing $N=16$ artifact (three
FEASIBLE trials, all independently validated) and a new follow-up matrix at
$N=1,2,3,4,8$ with three trials per size. The follow-up contains 15 records:
9 FEASIBLE and 6 UNSAT records, with no timeout or error. Gurobi 13.0.3 was
reported in the record metadata. Its results are stored in
\texttt{results/raw/benchmark\_gurobi\_followup.json} and the corresponding
summary and CSV files. FEASIBLE denotes a valid found placement; it is not
renamed to OPTIMAL.

\section{Discussion and limitations}
The comparison uses one machine, one run process, three repetitions, and
different model paradigms. Solver startup and model construction affect total
wall time. CPLEX Community Edition limits the MIP comparison at larger sizes.
OR-Tools measurements do not exist because the package is unavailable. SVG figures require a LaTeX setup
that supports SVG or prior conversion to PDF/PNG; conversion was not performed
because no LaTeX toolchain was available.

\section{Conclusion and future work}
The project now has independently validated SAT, CPLEX MIP, CPLEX CP, and
Gurobi adapters and a reproducible multi-solver benchmark. Future work is to
repeat the comparison in a licensed full CPLEX environment and, if required by
the assignment, with OR-Tools installed using the same schema and timeout policy.
